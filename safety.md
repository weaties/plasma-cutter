# Safety: read before the first live cut

Plasma cutting throws molten metal and sparks, makes metal fume, and runs high voltage over
compressed air. These are the minimums for this build, so check them against the plans and the
cutter's manual.

## Where
- **Not in a room with carpet, papers, sawdust or a Shaper/woodworking dust collector.** A plasma table and a woodshop do not share air or floor: sparks travel metres, and fine wood dust burns.
- A concrete floor, nothing flammable within ~10 m of the spark stream, or a full-height metal enclosure.
- **Water table** (slats over a water tray) to catch sparks and trap most of the fume, or a downdraft table with a spark arrestor.

## Fume
- Extract to outdoors. Never cut galvanised, painted or plated steel without strong extraction and a respirator: zinc fume causes metal fume fever.
- Wear a P100 respirator when cutting without extraction or when cleaning the water tray.

## Fire
- A CO₂ or ABC extinguisher within reach, a metal bin for offcuts, and nobody leaves the table for an hour after the last cut (hot slag smoulders).

## Electrical
- **Low-frequency / blowback-start cutter only**: high-frequency start interferes with and kills controllers and laptops.
- Follow the plans' grounding and shielding: the work-clamp ground goes to the slats, and the machine frame and the electronics get a separate earth. Use shielded stepper cables.
- Keep the control box away from the cutting area; put an e-stop where you can reach it standing at the table.

## Air
- Clean dry air: a refrigerated or desiccant dryer plus a filter at the cutter, because water in the air kills consumables and ruins the arc.

## PPE
- Shade 5-8 lenses (check the cutter's manual), leather gloves, cotton or wool clothing (no synthetics), and hearing protection.
