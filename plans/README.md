# plans/ (local only; git-ignored except this file)

The JD's Garage plans are online-only and paid-for. Keep the link and any local copies (STLs for
printing, notes on the drawings) here, outside git. Each builder uses the link from their own
purchase; ask JD before sharing it.

Suggested local layout:
```
plans/link.txt          the drive link from the purchase email
plans/stl/              the plans' STL files, downloaded for printing
plans/print-log.md      which STLs are printed, material, settings, fit notes
```

Printing the plans' parts: follow the plans' material and settings. Where they don't say, use
PETG, or ASA for anything near the torch or in the sun, with 4 walls and 40 % infill for brackets
that carry the gantry. Open the STL in Bambu Studio directly, since there's no need to go through SolidCraft.
